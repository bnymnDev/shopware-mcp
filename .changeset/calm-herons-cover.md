A product picture from a URL, the shop's trading settings, and an extension pack for FroshTools.

`product_cover_set` gives a product a cover image from a public URL (the shop downloads it) or from base64 bytes: media record in the product folder, upload, product media, cover, shown as one dry run of four requests. `shop_settings` reads an allowlist of core system-config domains (basic information, login and registration, cart, listing, tax, newsletter, addresses, documents), shop-wide or per sales channel with inheritance, and drops credential-like keys.

When the open-source FroshTools plugin is active, three read-only tools appear: `frosh_health` (platform health and performance checks), `frosh_queue` (message queue transports and waiting messages) and `frosh_composer_audit` (dependency advisories).
